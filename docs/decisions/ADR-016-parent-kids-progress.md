# ADR-016: Parent-managed child profile and progress

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-06

- Ages: 6–8 or 9–12 bands.
- Kids free anonymous trial.
- Optional parent Google/Apple account to manage child profiles; one adult account can also use the adult learning product.
- Child profile minimum: nickname, age band, learning progress. Do not require legal name or exact birth date.
- Store progress locally first. Optional encrypted export/import; single selected active cloud backup target, Google Drive appDataFolder or iCloud private database.
- Account identity and cloud storage authorization are separate grants. Cloud providers do not automatically share data.
- Avoid transferring child profiles to Devnetra servers by default. Privacy review required before release.
